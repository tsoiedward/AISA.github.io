About AISA

AI Safety Asia (AISA) is a global non-profit dedicated to the international governance of safe AI by Asia, starting in Southeast Asia.

We strive to develop interoperable and inclusive AI governance systems across Asia and bridge Global North-South gaps in AI governance. We advance our mission through regional talent capacity-building, conducting actionable policy research, and facilitating intergenerational dialogue. We believe that a future of safe and governed AI in Asia will mitigate catastrophic risks, reduce adverse societal impacts, and unlock the next frontier in equitable socioeconomic development for 60% of the global population.

Why AISA?

  • Southeast Asia lags behind in AI preparedness, creating an "algorithmic divide" that leaves the region vulnerable to AI's adverse impacts and missing out on its benefits.
  • Despite significant investments from the U.S. and China in Southeast Asian AI startups, local capacity and governance remain weak.
  • AISA addresses this gap by developing local talent and creating region-specific AI governance frameworks.

Our Goals

Enhance AI Safety Governance: Build local expertise and foster collaboration to produce practical AI safety policies.

Upskill Civil Servants: Equip mid-career civil servants with AI safety knowledge and tools to inform policymaking.

Incubate Junior Researchers: Develop junior policy researchers' skills through mentorship and practical policy input.

Foster Collaboration: Encourage cross-disciplinary and cross-regional collaboration to generate Asia-focused AI safety research.

AISA  Assembly

AISA convenes critical stakeholders for urgent discussions on international AI governance across Southeast Asia. Our signature initiative is the AI Safety Roundtable Series, which focuses on regional opportunities, challenges, and strategies for AI safety governance.

Since 2024, we have conducted AI Safety Roundtables in Manila, Jakarta, Singapore, and Kuala Lumpur and Bangkok. Speakers have included former Ministers, high-level government officials, senior academics, and AI experts from think tanks and international organisations.

AISA Governance Academy

AISA leads AI governance capacity-building in Southeast Asia through the incubation of promising policy researchers and mid-senior level civil servants across ASEAN countries, starting with the Philippines and Indonesia.

Our pilot program, AISA Scholars, is a 12-week fellowship in AI Safety and Governance for junior researchers and government staff in Southeast Asia. Participants will receive the knowledge, skills, and connections to conduct high quality AI policy research, manage key risks proactively, and make informed governance decisions.

AISA Policy Lab

AISA conducts and publishes actionable policy research on the international governance of AI to contribute insights from the Global AI Majority.

Our Southeast Asia Policy Observatory (SEA Observatory) provides a comprehensive overview of the state of AI policy and governance in the region. It offers global policymakers and policy researchers a tool to draft more informed and safer AI policies, and supports AI policy labs in understanding the regulatory landscape for more focused impact.

The SEA Observatory builds on the AISA Scholars program to form an incubation hub for policy researchers to conduct regionally-focused research, which will serve as policy advice for Southeast Asian governments.
